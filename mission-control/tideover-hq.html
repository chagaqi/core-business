Proof-only doctrine (never bends)

No fabricated metrics, testimonials, clients, or results. Confidence bands, never hard delivery dates (enforced in code at draft AND send). Literal [CASE STUDY PLACEHOLDER] until a real cohort completes. Third-party benchmarks live in cited popovers, never beside merchant data. Demo surfaces carry a persistent "Sample data" watermark. "Self-improving" appears only as measured numbers with n-badges, never as the word "learning".
