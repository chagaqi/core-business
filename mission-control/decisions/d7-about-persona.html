2 · The ABOUT section (sales page, long version)

Proposed replacement

I'm Dylan, the founder of Tideover. Let me tell you what I am and what I'm not, because trust is the entire product here.

I'm not an ex-support manager, and I've never shipped a Kickstarter. I'm a technical founder who spent months studying why presale support fails: the card-network dispute rules most merchants have never read (the clock runs 120 days past your expected delivery date, not your sale date), Kickstarter's own creator guidance on delay communication, and the QA scorecards professional support teams grade themselves with. Then I built all of it into software instead of a slide deck.

So Tideover's reassurance scripts aren't my war stories, and that's the point. They operationalize what's documented to work: the platform's published guidance (update steadily — silence, not delay, is what breaks backer trust), the support industry's own quality standards, and your real inbox once we start. And the software audits every reply before it sends: it physically cannot promise a hard delivery date. I didn't trust instinct with your customers, so I built a system that doesn't need it.

Now the part most vendors skip. Tideover is new. A real "we cut refunds X%" case study needs a complete 60–120 day presale cycle to even exist. It doesn't exist yet, and I won't fake one. No invented stats, no borrowed logos, no fictional testimonials. If I'd lie about my own proof, you couldn't trust me with your customers.

What you get in the pilot are the honest leading indicators, measured against your own captured baseline: faster first responses on presale tickets, fewer repeat "where's my order" messages, and every save logged with the actual ticket. The full refund case study lands after your first cohort finishes its wait — and you'll never pay for proof that isn't there yet. That's the founding-partner deal: you get the work free while I earn the case study, on your real orders.
