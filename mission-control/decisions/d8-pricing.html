The rules the page states out loud (each one is a wedge vs competitors)

  • Flat price. No per-ticket meter, no per-AI-resolution fee (Gorgias bills both — cited, on their own pricing pages)
  • No setup fee (TalentPop charges ~$2,500)
  • Unlimited seats — your VA works in the cockpit free
  • Month-to-month; pause between campaign cycles — presale is seasonal, billing respects that
  • No performance fees until real case studies exist (proof-only)
