## BLOCKERS

**B1 — S9 "where most partners land after proof" implies a customer base that does not exist.** The on-page proof pledge in `Operator.tsx` literally says the case study "lands only after a real cohort completes" — i.e. cohort one hasn't run. A pricing card labeled by where "most partners land" fabricates modal behavior of customers you don't have. This is a testimonial-shaped implication and it ships dishonest. Must relabel to a self-sourced recommendation or a trigger condition (no crowd claim).

**B2 — S3 integration logo row collides head-on with the committed on-page pledge "No borrowed logo."** `Operator.tsx` line 51 pledges "No invented metric. No fake testimonial. No borrowed logo." A monochrome third-party logo band sitting in the credibility slot "right after the product feels real" is *visually indistinguishable from a "trusted by" endorsement bar* — a sharp visitor reads it as contradiction. The integration/endorsement distinction is real to you, invisible to them. Fix: (a) reword the pledge to "no borrowed customer logo," AND (b) present integrations unmistakably as capability — small inline "Connects with" chips in a functional context, captioned, not a monochrome trust-band in the social-proof position. Also: only show marks for integrations that actually ship today (a logo for a planned integration is an implied-capability claim).

**B3 — Terracotta as the S4 "dispute-risk" gradient endpoint breaks the single-action-accent discipline.** The plan itself commits "terracotta plays coral's exact role: every CTA + the one spot color… no fourth solid." Using terracotta to *also* mean "danger/dispute" across the tide-line means the color stops reliably signaling "click here," and it collides with the product's own red/amber/green risk pills. Warm the tide-line teal → tan/amber; reserve terracotta for actions only.

## IMPROVEMENTS
- **Paper-device overload.** The plan stacks 7 paper mechanisms (torn edges, strata, grain, corner-folds, stacked double-edge, wave dividers, parallax) + 6 nautical objects (boat/buoy/lighthouse/tide-line/strata/anchor). That fights the "calm" register and risks theme-park-nautical kitsch. Pick ~3 signature devices; lean on tide/wave + one hero object, not lighthouse+anchor+buoy+boat all at once.
- **S2 deep-link `#demo?scene=refund-risk` is malformed** (query after hash won't parse). Use `?scene=refund-risk#demo` or a parsed-hash client handler.
- **Parallax/`scroll-timeline`** has Safari/Firefox gaps — make it explicit progressive enhancement over the IntersectionObserver path, off under reduced-motion (plan hedges; commit to it).
- **`feTurbulence` grain** should be a small fixed repeating tile, not a full-viewport filter, to avoid paint cost on low-end mobile.
- **S3 founder numbers appear outside the Operator first-person frame.** Keep "two years / 60-plus-day" explicitly attributed to Dylan in the strip, or the pledge's "only numbers on this page" logic frays.
- **S5 embed:** `/app` is already no-auth sample-data, so the interactive embed is feasible — but it *reads* hot files (`inbox/page.tsx`, `DraftRail`, `ApprovalBar`). Serialize + verify per CLAUDE.md, and ship the labeled looping capture as the day-one fallback so the homepage never blocks on the embed.
