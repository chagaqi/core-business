## 3. When NOT to follow up

- They replied at all, even "not interested" — that's a signal, don't add a second touch.
- They opened more than once but didn't click or reply — active consideration, let it sit past the window instead of pushing.
- The preorder/lead-time finding may be stale (product shipped, page changed) — recheck before sending or the specific becomes a lie.
- One follow-up, ever. No third message on this thread.
