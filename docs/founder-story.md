## Raw account (as given)

Started a gym equipment business during peak COVID, when the gyms closed. Closed **$200K in
orders in week 1** — then had to tide the customers over for **60+ days** during the biggest
shipping delays in e-commerce history. Ran it for **2 full years** and learned the ins and outs
of what keeps a customer happy and what prevents refunds/chargebacks.

In his words: "if I was asked to wave a magic wand and the stress of handling the onslaught of
inquiries — when you know there's nothing you can do but convince them to wait a bit longer —
could just go away in an instant, that would have saved me my sanity, my nervous system, my
family and my time to focus on growing the business. That's where Tide Over was born."
