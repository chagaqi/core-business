# FIRST-CASH RUNBOOK
### From "production-ready" to "$3–5K cash collected" — solo founder, proof-only, 4×24, own-pipeline (Sora 2 + kie.ai)

**Scope of this pack:** the precise, ordered sequence to collect the **first $3–5K in cash** — not MRR, not $20K, just real money in the Stripe account. This is the cash sprint that funds the rest. The bottleneck this pack is built around is **production skill** — every step is designed to accelerate it, not assume it.

**The one rule that shapes everything:** A priority prospect never gets a cold email without a rendered ad *of their own product* already in it. The made-for-them sample is the proof — there are no case studies. That makes **sample-production time the true rate-limiter**, which is why we practice on the 3 fictional brands first and only render real-prospect samples once the renders are good enough to send.

---

## 0. The gate before the runbook starts: "production-ready"

Do **not** start the outreach sequence until you can clear this self-test, because a rough render sent to a real A-tier founder burns a scarce lead permanently (only 24 A-tier brands exist).

**Production-ready = you can, on one of the 3 fictional brands:**
1. Mint a consistent Sora 2 character holding a pixel-perfect product (label doesn't warp across 4+ reused clips) — per `28-sora2-consistent-character-workflow.md` §5-step.
2. Batch 5+ clips through kie.ai reusing the tagged character, changing only action + dialogue.
3. Caption/hook-card/export to 9:16 + 4:5, FTC-clean (no disease/enhancement claims, no fabricated testimonial).
4. Produce a 15-second spec ad in **≤20–30 min** start-to-export. (This number is load-bearing: the whole funnel's throughput rides on it — see §2.)

**Done-when:** You show a render to 3 people who don't know it's AI and none of them say "that looks fake/AI." If they do, you are not production-ready — keep practicing on fictional brands. This *is* the bottleneck; respect it.

## 1. The exact ordered sequence (each with a concrete done-when)

The order is dependency-driven. Steps 1–3 are build; 4–7 are the live motion. The long pole (email domain warmup, ~2–3 weeks) starts in parallel on Day 0 and is *not* on the critical path to first cash if you bridge with DMs (see §6 and Stall #1).

### STEP 1 — Render the portfolio (proof-of-capability reel)
- **What:** Render **10–15 finished ads** from the 72 fictional-brand scripts in `19-creative-scripts.md` — span all 3 brands (MycoMornings, ZenChews, DailyGreens24) and all 4 angles (PAS, FND, MECH, TRAN), and at least 3 formats (TH, VO, LF). This is also where your production skill crosses from "practice" to "portfolio."
- **Why first:** It's the only proof you can show before any client exists, it doubles as deliberate practice, and it builds the reusable per-brand character/product assets and brand-kit setups that make later real samples faster.
- **Done-when:** 10–15 ads, captioned, exported 9:16+4:5, FTC-clean, named `BRAND_ANGLE_HOOK_FORMAT_LEN_vNN`, sitting in a portfolio folder/landing page. Each one passes the §3.3 QA gate (lipsync locks on hook, no warped label, ingredient names pronounced right).

### STEP 2 — Stand up the go-live links (the minimal stack)
- **What:** Deploy the landing page free, wire in a Stripe payment link ($497 pack), a Calendly booking link, and one sending channel. (Full stack spec in §3.)
- **Why second:** A reply with no place to book a call or pay = a dropped lead. The links must exist before the first send.
- **Done-when:** You can (a) open the public landing page, (b) click a Stripe link and reach a real $497 checkout, (c) book a test slot on Calendly, (d) confirm all `EDIT_ME`/`[FILL]` markers are resolved (real slot count, real email, real links — no fabricated numbers anywhere).

### STEP 3 — Render made-for-them samples for the top ~10 A-tier leads
- **What:** For the **top 10 A-tier brands**, render the specific made-for-them ad named in `22-prospect-personalization.md` (the angle/SKU is already chosen per brand, e.g. Kiala Carnival Candy taste-test, Spacegoods Rainbow Dust ritual, First Person Sunbeam founder-story). 8 of these already have shootable scripts in `26-prospect-sample-scripts.md`.
- **Why now:** This is the proof spine of every cold email. It's also the single biggest time cost in the whole runbook (~15–30 min each = ~3–5 hrs for 10). Templatize: reuse the fixed 15-sec spec format and brand-kit setup so each is a fast variation, not a custom build.
- **Done-when:** 10 rendered, FTC-clean samples, each hosted at a clean shareable `{{sampleLink}}` (host with view-analytics so you can see if it got watched). Protect these — never send a rough one; re-render until it clears the "doesn't look AI" bar.

### STEP 4 — Enrich emails + send the openers
- **What:** Run the 10 leads' founder names+domains through enrichment (every lead-list row is flagged `enrich` — **never fabricate an email**). Paste the verified email + the rendered `{{sampleLink}}` into the pre-written opener from `22-prospect-personalization.md`. Send. Sequence the follow-ups (4-email cadence from `14-cold-email-sequence.md`), routed by Track (Wounded Vet vs Volume-Lean — already assigned per lead).
- **Why:** This is the ignition. The opener is already written per brand; the only two missing fields are the verified email and the sample link.
- **Done-when:** 10 priority sends out with sample-in-email; follow-up sequence loaded; replies routing to an inbox you watch daily. (Parallel: as domains finish warming, widen to the rest of the 24 A-tier at 30–50/day.)

### STEP 5 — Discovery calls
- **What:** Reply → book → run the 15-min discovery script in `18-sales-playbook.md`. The call's first job is **spend** (decides retainer vs pack-first branch); second is **Track** (Refuge vs Leverage). You are never cold — they've already seen their own product as a UGC ad.
- **Why:** The call converts curiosity (the sample) into intent (commitment).
- **Done-when:** Each call ends in one of: (a) founding-partner pilot committed, (b) $497 pack sold, (c) retainer quoted + verbal yes, or (d) honest disqualify. Log the verdict.

### STEP 6 — Close founding partners
- **What:** For fits, land the close: present the guarantee (beat their control on hook/hold-rate, CPA flat in 30 days, or next cycle free), state the *real* remaining founding-slot count, and branch the ask:
  - **$20–100K/mo spend** → quote retainer live (mid $1.5–2.5K / standard $2.5–3.5K), offer founding terms (locked rate + the $0 instrumented 5-video pilot before any paid commitment).
  - **$5–20K/mo** → put the **$497 pack** in their hand (foot-in-door, one-time).
  - Either way, capture the **control baseline before the pilot** (their current best ad's hook/hold-rate) — no baseline = no case study.
- **Done-when:** Signed pilot agreement (`24-pilot-agreement-and-intake.md`) and/or a paid Stripe checkout completed. Baseline screenshot captured.

### STEP 7 — Collect
- **What:** Take the money. $497 pack = immediate cash via the Stripe link. First retainer = invoice the first month up front (month-to-month, paid before the cycle ships). Any paid founding-partner fee = collected on signature.
- **Done-when:** **$3–5K cleared in Stripe.** That's the finish line of this pack.

## 2. Funnel math — backward from $3–5K

### 2.1 The honest assumptions (falsify against real data after the first 50–100 sends)

These are the launch-stage (zero case studies) bands from `25-path-to-20k-model.md`. Plan with the **floor**, because proof inventory is empty.

| Stage | Range | Plan-with | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Priority send → reply | 8–15% | **10%** | Sample-in-email + true hook beats generic 1–3%; no proof caps the ceiling |
| Reply → call/pilot interest | 30–40% | **35%** | Many replies are "nice, not now" |
| Call → pilot **or** $497 pack | 40–55% | **45%** | Free pilot is low-friction; pack is impulse-yes (<2.5% of monthly spend) |
| Pilot/pack → first retainer | 25–40% | **30%** | Offer doc's own floor; lifts toward 40% only after case studies exist |

### 2.2 What it takes to clear $3–5K (revenue mix)

$3–5K **cash** (not MRR) is reachable *before* a single case study exists, because it doesn't require many retainers. Three honest mixes:

| Mix | Components | Cash |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Pack-heavy (fastest, lowest-trust-dependency)** | 6 × $497 packs + 1 mid retainer first month ($2.0K) | **~$4,982** |
| **B — Balanced (most likely)** | 4 × $497 packs + 1 standard retainer first month ($3.0K) | **~$4,988** |
| **C — Retainer-light + paid pilot** | 2 × $497 packs + 1 mid retainer ($2.0K) + 1 paid founding-partner fee (~$1.0K) | **~$3,994** |

**Note on the $0 pilots:** The free instrumented pilots are *not* cash — they're the proof-manufacturing engine for later. They convert to retainers at ~30% *after* the first cycle delivers a delta (~Day 45+). So the first $3–5K leans on **$497 packs + first-month retainer invoices**, with pilots seeded in parallel to feed the *next* tranche of cash.

### 2.3 The send/call/close volumes (Mix B, the planning case)

Work backward from **4 packs + 1 retainer = 5 closes** that produce cash. A pack closes off a call; a retainer closes off a call (often after a pilot, but the first-month invoice can land on the call's "standard retainer, founding rate" yes).

Treating all 5 as "call → paid commitment" at the 45% call-conversion:

```
5 paid commitments  ÷ 0.45 (call → pack/commit)   = ~11 qualified calls
11 calls            ÷ 0.35 (reply → call)          = ~32 positive replies
32 replies          ÷ 0.10 (send → reply)          = ~315 priority sample-backed sends
```

**Reality check against the lead list:** 315 sends > the 24 A-tier brands and even strains the full 79-brand list at one touch each. But each lead gets a **4-email sequence**, so 79 brands × 4 ≈ **316 touches** — almost exactly the requirement. **This means: to net $3–5K you must work essentially the entire A+B-tier list through the full sequence, and your top-10 A-tier samples must over-perform** (priority sample-backed sends reply closer to the 12–15% top of the band, not 10%). If the top-10 A-tier convert at the high end, you can hit $3–5K on **~120–180 sends** instead of 315.

**Honest planning numbers to carry:**
- **Optimistic (top-10 A-tier reply at ~15%, packs close well):** ~120–150 sends, ~6–8 calls → $3–5K.
- **Plan-with (10% reply):** ~300–320 sends (the full sequenced list) → $3–5K.
- **Pessimistic (proof gap bites, 7% reply / 25% pilot→retainer):** the list *starves* before $5K — you reach ~$2–3K (mostly packs) and must replenish leads (+40 brands) to finish.

**The single biggest swing factor is sample quality** (reply rate) — which loops straight back to production skill. Better renders = fewer sends needed for the same cash.

## 5. Founding-partner → paid conversion path

The free pilot is the **proof factory**, not the cash. Here's how it becomes the first paid retainer + the case study:

1. **Offer the $0 instrumented pilot** (5 videos) only to a qualified $20–100K/mo fit on the call. Framing: "I'm taking a small number of founding partners; in exchange for the right to publish your before/after numbers and a one-line testimonial, you get founding terms." State the *real* slot count.
2. **Capture the control baseline FIRST** — their current best ad's hook-rate + hold-rate, screenshotted, before anything ships. **This is the non-negotiable step**: no baseline = no case study = the pilot was wasted proof.
3. **Sign the pilot agreement** (`24-pilot-agreement-and-intake.md`) + run the brand-brief intake (`production-sop.md` §1) — collect the SKU product-prompt once; it's reused every cycle.
4. **Deliver the cycle** — run the 5 videos (or a full 24-pack for the strongest fit) through the 4×24 line; launch and tag in *their* account, *their* naming convention.
5. **Measure the delta at Day 14** — control hold-rate → winner hold-rate, CPA flat-or-better, head-to-head in their own dashboard. This delta is **case study #1** (e.g. "control 1.1% → winner 1.9% hold-rate, +73%, CPA flat").
6. **Convert on the data** — the retainer ask now has proof on the table: "Here's your delta. The retainer is where this runs every two weeks before fatigue kills the winner." Pilot→retainer ≈ 30% pre-proof, drifting to 40% once you have 3 deltas. Invoice month 1 up front = recurring cash begins.
7. **Bank the proof** — retire one `[CASE STUDY PLACEHOLDER]`, insert the real delta into the cold-email sequence and sell sheet. Each documented win lifts Perceived Likelihood (6→8) and re-rates the *whole* funnel's reply and close rates upward.

**Cash timing nuance:** Because the pilot is free and its delta lands ~Day 14 of the cycle, **pilot-driven retainer cash arrives later** than pack cash. So the first $3–5K leans on **packs + any founding-partner that signs a paid retainer directly off the call** (sample was strong enough that they skip the free pilot); the pilots feed the *second* wave.

## 6. Realistic timeline (which week each $ milestone lands) + top 3 stalls

### Timeline (honest, warmup-corrected)

| Week | Focus | $ milestone |
|---|---|---|
| **Week 0** | Start email-domain warmup (Day 0, the long pole). Render the 10–15 portfolio ads. Stand up Stripe/Calendly/LP. | $0 — build |
| **Week 1** | Render top-10 A-tier made-for-them samples. Stand up the sample host. Begin **DM outreach** to top 10 (no warmup needed). | $0 — pipeline |
| **Week 2** | DMs → first replies → first discovery calls. First **$497 packs** start landing (pack is an impulse-yes on a call). | **First cash: ~$497–$1.5K** (1–3 packs) |
| **Week 3** | Email domains warm → widen to full A-tier at 30–50/day. More calls. First **founding pilots committed** (baselines captured). First **retainer quoted + verbal yes**, month-1 invoiced. | **~$2–3.5K cumulative** (packs + first retainer first-month) |
| **Week 4–5** | Work B-tier through the sequence. Close 1–2 more packs and/or the second retainer first-month. Replenish leads (+40) if the list is thinning. | **$3–5K cleared — pack target hit** |
| **(Week 6–7)** | First pilot cycle delivers → case study #1 → pilot→retainer conversions begin (the *next* cash wave, and the start of MRR). | Recurring cash begins |

**Read:** First cash ~**Week 2** (packs via DMs), the **$3–5K target lands ~Week 4–5**. This is ~1–2 weeks later than a naïve "Day 21" because the email warmup clock is real — which is exactly why the first cash rides **DMs to the top 10**, not the email rig.

### Top 3 things most likely to stall first cash (and the fix)

1. **Production skill / sample quality not over the bar (HIGHEST — this is your stated bottleneck).** If renders read as "AI slop" — warped labels, uncanny lipsync, drifting character — reply rates collapse from 10% toward 2–4% and you'd need 800+ sends you don't have leads for. *Fix:* Do **not** start real-lead outreach until you clear the §0 production-ready gate on the fictional brands. Lock the Sora 2 consistent-character method (product embedded at generation time, character minted-and-reused, change only action+dialogue). Templatize to ≤20 min/sample so quality is repeatable, not a one-off. Protect the 24 A-tier — never send a rough render.

2. **Email warmup gates outreach → no first cash until ~Day 30 (HIGH, timing-fatal).** Domains can't send volume for 2–3 weeks; waiting on them pushes first cash a month out. *Fix:* Bridge weeks 1–3 with **LinkedIn/X DMs + warm intros** to the top 10 — no warmup required, and you only need 10 priority touches to start landing packs. Start the email warmup Day 0 in parallel so the *full-list* motion is ready by Week 3.

3. **Lead-list starvation / proof gap throttles conversion (MEDIUM-HIGH).** 79 brands × 4 emails ≈ 316 touches yields maybe 1–2 retainers at 10% reply — and with zero case studies, pilot→retainer sits at the 30% floor, so the funnel can run dry around Week 4–5 before $5K clears. *Fix:* (a) Make the first cash **pack-weighted** (Mix A/B) — packs close on the sample alone and don't need proof. (b) Stand up lead-gen as a **standing weekly process** (+40 qualified functional-consumables brands/month) so the list doesn't starve. (c) Race the **first founding-partner baseline + cycle** to manufacture case study #1 fast — it re-rates every downstream rate and is the highest-ROI activity once the first packs are in.

## Bottom line

- **$3–5K is a cash number, not MRR** — reachable **before** any case study, leaning on **$497 packs + first-month retainer invoices**, with free pilots seeded in parallel to feed the next wave.
- **Plan ~120–320 priority sample-backed sends** depending on how good the renders are — sample quality (your production skill) is the dominant swing factor, so the runbook front-loads practice on the 3 fictional brands.
- **First cash ~Week 2 via DMs to the top 10; $3–5K cleared ~Week 4–5.** The email rig and its warmup are stood up in parallel but are *not* the critical path to the first few thousand.
- **The three stalls are, in order: render quality, warmup timing, lead/proof starvation** — and the fixes are, respectively: clear the production-ready gate first, bridge with DMs, and make the first cash pack-weighted while racing the first pilot baseline.
